## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated, meticulous world of premier forensic instrumentation, digital analysis, and chain-of-custody documentation. It caters to legal institutions, forensic investigators, senior researchers, and judicial authorities who demand absolute unimpeachability, quiet luxury, and microscopic accuracy. 

The aesthetic synthesizes **Forensic Laboratory Rigor** with **Editorial Modern Minimalism**. It deliberately avoids sensationalized crime fiction tropes, blood spatters, or dark cinematic kitsch. Instead, it positions evidence as an artifact of purest truth: quiet, illuminated under surgical-grade spectra, systematically indexed, and objectively documented. Interfaces resemble calibrated analytical workstations with subtle coordinate crosshairs, optical reticle markings, and precise structural framing. The emotional tone is authoritative, unshakeable, pristine, and luminous.

## Colors

The palette operates exclusively within a high-density, low-luminance canvas that eliminates visual glare while isolating evidentiary focus points.

- **Canvas & Base Foundations:** Deep graphite-charcoal (`#0B1117`) serves as the core void, layered up through `#111C26` for primary panels and `#16232E` for hovered or elevated state surfaces.
- **Precision Scientific Accent (`#00E5C9`):** Represents high-resolution diagnostic readout states, laser spectrographic analysis, active scans, and primary focal points. Complemented by analytical cyan (`#0EA5E9`) for data visualisations, telemetry, and link trails.
- **Evidence Index Tag (`#E2C376`):** Reserved strictly for specimen taxonomy, custody verification stamps, authentication seals, and validated status flags. It lends a legacy, archival weight against the digital teal.
- **Structural Lines (`#223344`):** Delicate sub-pixel boundary definition for coordinate grids, divider rules, and data enclosures.
- **Typography Levels:** Pure luminous off-white (`#F3F6F8`) ensures crystalline readability against the deep graphite void, while muted slate (`#A7B4BF`) handles secondary metadata, field guides, and custody timestamps.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy balances high-efficiency modern legibility with rigorous data parsing. 

- **Primary Editorial Layer (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Drives headline statements and portfolio case titles with sculpted modern terminals, conveying high-end curatorial poise.
- **Narrative & Observational Layer (Inter):** Deploys tabular figures, neutral neutralism, and optimal optical sizing across body descriptions, analysis summaries, and formal testimonies.
- **Instrumentation & Verification Layer (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for technical identifiers, specimen hashes, optical coordinates, GPS coordinates, calibration metrics, and chronometric custody logs. Monospaced elements must always render in uppercase or tabular sequences to reinforce evidentiary authenticity.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout operates on an explicit 12-column architectural grid punctuated by technical sub-grids.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column dynamic container with 48px margins and 24px gutters. Outer margins accommodate non-intrusive datum lines (axis indices, timestamp rules, coordinate ticks `01 // 12`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 16px gutters. Secondary analytical sidebars collapse into stacked tabbed drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column flow with 20px margins and 12px gutters. Coordinate indicators align horizontally atop cards instead of lateral axis margins.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px modular baseline. Structural dividers between data segments utilize razor-thin 1px outlines (`#223344`) running full bleed through gutters to evoke surgical layout dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, blurred drop shadows which soften and obscure clean forensic boundaries. Instead, visual hierarchy is articulated through **Tonal Surface Hierarchy** and **Optical Backlight Illumination**:

- **Ground Layer (Elevation 0 - `#0B1117`):** The primary environment canvas. May feature micro-opacity (4%) orthogonal vector cross-grids simulating a calibrated stage.
- **Inspection Tier (Elevation 1 - `#111C26`):** Case files, evidentiary dossiers, and persistent panels. Outlined with a 1px solid border of `#223344`.
- **Focus & Interaction Tier (Elevation 2 - `#16232E`):** Focused metrics modules, hover states, and active specimen viewers. Accented by a razor-thin top perimeter line in primary teal at 30% opacity (`rgba(0, 229, 201, 0.3)`) and a focused, narrow cold glow (`0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 229, 201, 0.04)`).
- **Critical Overlay Tier (Elevation 3 - `#16232E`):** Modal inspection dialogs and full-magnification lightbox viewers. Accompanied by a 1px perimeter border of `#00E5C9` at 50% opacity and an absolute backdrop filter blur of `12px` over an 85% opacity void.

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, engineered, and restrained.

- **Micro-radii (`roundedness: 1`):** Core interactive elements, tags, buttons, and specimen enclosures feature a tight 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius. This prevents the harshness of raw brutalism while retaining the precision of cut glass, laser optics, and industrial silicon hardware.
- **Corner Notches & Calibrations:** Specialized data frames and evidentiary plaques may utilize asymmetric 45-degree chamfered corner cuts (4px) or hair-thin bracket markers on top-left and bottom-right edges to signify sealed custody documentation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action/Analyze):** Deep graphite foundation (`#111C26`) bordered by 1px solid teal (`#00E5C9`), primary text in `#00E5C9`. On hover, fills with `#00E5C9`, text shifts to `#0B1117`, accompanied by a 4px localized ambient glow. Font: `JetBrains Mono`, 12px, letter-spacing `0.06em`, all caps.
- **Secondary (Inspect/Logs):** Translucent background (`#16232E`), 1px border `#223344`, text `#F3F6F8`. On hover, border shifts to `#0EA5E9`, text to `#0EA5E9`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Zero border, monospaced text in `#A7B4BF` preceded by `[+]` or `->` glyph indicators.

### Evidence Tags & Chips
- **Chain of Custody Badge:** Pill or micro-box (`0.25rem` radius), background `rgba(226, 195, 118, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 195, 118, 0.4)`, text `#E2C376`. Displays `JetBrains Mono` identifier prefix (e.g., `REF. #804-A`).
- **Telemetry/Status Chip:** Micro circular indicator light (4px solid `#00E5C9` with pulsing soft glow) followed by uppercase status string in `label-caps`.

### Cards & Dossier Modules
- **Evidence Card:** Grounded in `#111C26` with `1px solid #223344`. Top edge contains a monospaced serial header bar separated by a 1px line. Card transitions to `#16232E` on hover with a top-border highlight in `#00E5C9`. No organic motion; state transitions use snappy 150ms linear curves.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Analytical Field:** Inset fill (`#0B1117`), 1px border (`#223344`), active typing indicator in `#00E5C9`. Monospaced placeholder text in `#A7B4BF` at 60% opacity. Focused input displays coordinate reticles `[ ]` at right-hand bounds.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Precision squares and diamonds (14px × 14px) with 1px border (`#223344`). Active state fills interior with `#00E5C9` with an inner 2px dark boundary gap.

### Specialty Components
- **Spectrogram & Metrics Bar:** Horizontal metric scales utilizing sub-divided tick indicators instead of smooth slider tracks, emphasizing discrete numerical increments.
- **Custody Timeline Tree:** Vertical ruled line in `#223344` connecting chronological evidence transfers, marked by calibrated reticle nodes that illuminate in `#00E5C9` upon active verification.
